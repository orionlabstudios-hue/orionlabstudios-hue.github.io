Cloud Chase Privacy Policy

Sep 28, 2026 · Orion Lab Studios

The short version

Cloud Chase is built to collect as little as possible. This policy applies from the day the game launches.

  • The story mode and the runs send nothing about you. Your progress is saved on your own phone.
  • We ask your birth month and year once. We keep only an age group on your phone and never send it anywhere.
  • Cloud Duel, our online mode, is for players 16 and over. Unity runs it for us using an anonymous player ID, a made-up name and your friends list. There is no real name, no email and no chat.
  • The world ranking shows the best endless runs. If you are 16 or over, your best Eternal Wake distances go to it with the anonymous player ID and a name you choose for the board. Under 16, nothing about you is sent: you still see the top 100, and the game works out on your phone where you would place.
  • There are no ads in this version.
  • You can delete your Cloud Duel data in Settings at any time. We do not sell your data.

Who we are

Cloud Chase is made by Orion Lab Studios ("we", "us"). For the personal data described here, we are the "controller": we decide how it is used.

Contact us about privacy, or to delete your data, at orionlabstudios@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

We do not use analytics, crash reporting or Unity's engine diagnostics. We never ask for your real name, email, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location, and there is no chat. The only thing you can type that other players see is your name on the world ranking: it is checked against a list of words that are not allowed, and any player can report it or block it. Reports come to us by email, and we remove names that break the rules.

Children

Cloud Chase is made for players 13 and over, but it protects younger players who find it anyway.

  • At first launch, before any online code starts, the game asks for your birth month and year. Only the resulting age group stays on the phone.
  • Under 16: Cloud Duel is switched off, so no Unity account is created. The world ranking still shows the top 100, from a public file that carries nothing about the player; their own runs are never sent and they cannot type a name for the board.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under 16 in the EU.
  • If you are a parent and think your child's data reached us, email orionlabstudios@gmail.com. We will delete it.

Who we share data with

We share personal data with one partner, and nobody else:

  • Unity Technologies runs Cloud Duel and the world ranking for us: sign-in, friends, parties, race connections and the ranking's names and distances. It acts on our instructions (Unity privacy policy).

Our website, including the public ranking file, is hosted by GitHub. Like any website host it sees the IP address of each device that loads a page or the file (GitHub privacy statement). We receive nothing from those visits.

Names on the world ranking are public: other players, and anyone who reads the public ranking file, can see them with their distances.

We do not sell personal data.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect players and the game from fraud or abuse.

Your choices and rights

You can act on your data without writing to anyone:

  • Delete your Cloud Duel data: Settings > Delete my Cloud Duel data. This deletes your Unity account, your name, your friends list, the players you blocked, and your name and distances on the world ranking.
  • Change or report a name on the world ranking: your own name from the Ranking screen; anyone else's with Report on their row.
  • Everything else: email orionlabstudios@gmail.com and we answer within 30 days.

Your rights by region:

  • EU, UK and Switzerland:
    • You can see, correct, delete, restrict, move, or object to the use of your personal data.
    • You can withdraw consent at any time.
    • You can complain to your local data protection authority.
  • California and other US states with privacy laws: you can know, delete and correct your data. We never treat you differently for using these rights.

Security, transfers and changes

  • Security: Cloud Duel sign-in travels over HTTPS, and race data is encrypted. We collect only what each feature needs, and restrict who on our team can reach the Unity dashboard.
  • Transfers: Unity may process data in the United States and other countries. Where EU or UK law requires it, those transfers use the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • Changes: if we change this policy, we update the date at the top. For important changes, we also tell you in the game before they apply.
