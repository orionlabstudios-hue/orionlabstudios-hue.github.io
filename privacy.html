Cloud Chase Privacy Policy

Sep 24, 2026 · Orion Lab Studios

The short version

Cloud Chase is built to collect as little as possible. This policy applies from the day the game launches.

  • The story mode and the runs send nothing about you. Your progress is saved on your own phone.
  • We ask your birth month and year once. We keep only an age group on your phone and never send it anywhere.
  • Cloud Duel, our online mode, is for players 16 and over. Unity runs it for us using an anonymous player ID, a made-up name and your friends list. There is no real name, no email and no chat.
  • The game shows ads. Players under 16 only see child-safe ads: never personalised, and using only the technical details needed to show them. Players 16 and over see personalised ads only if they agree.
  • You can delete your Cloud Duel data in Settings at any time. We do not sell your data.

Who we are

Cloud Chase is made by Orion Lab Studios ("we", "us"). For the personal data described here, we are the "controller": we decide how it is used.

Contact us about privacy, or to delete your data, at orionlabstudios@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

We do not use analytics, crash reporting or Unity's engine diagnostics. We never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location, and there is no chat.

Ads

Ads keep Cloud Chase free. Google AdMob shows them. Watching a rewarded ad, for a revive or a chest, is always your choice.

What every ad needs. To load and count an ad, the ad partner receives technical information: your IP address, device model and operating system, language, the app, and whether you viewed or tapped the ad. They use it to deliver the ad, count views, limit repeats and prevent fraud. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in funding a free game.

Personalised ads: 16 and over, and only if you agree. The ad partner may also use your device's advertising ID to show ads based on your interests.

  • In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, we ask with Google's consent form.
  • On iPhone, Apple's tracking prompt asks first.
  • If you say no, you still see ads, but they aren't personalised.
  • You can change your answer at any time in Settings > Privacy choices.

Players under 16 never get personalised ads. We tell the ad partner that the player is a child. It then uses no advertising ID and no tracking, and shows ads based only on the game itself. There is no tracking prompt.

How Google uses this data: Google's policy for partner apps.

Children

Cloud Chase is made for players 13 and over, but it protects younger players who find it anyway.

  • At first launch, before any online or ad code starts, the game asks for your birth month and year. Only the resulting age group stays on the phone.
  • Under 16:
    • Cloud Duel is switched off, so no Unity account is created.
    • Ads are child-safe and not personalised.
  • Under 13: ads are also flagged as "child-directed" under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Our ad partner, Google AdMob, may use the device's details only to deliver the ad, limit repeats and prevent fraud. It may never use them to build a profile or to target ads. How we make sure: every ad request from a player under 13 carries the child-directed flag, and we only use ad networks certified under Google Play's Families program, which bars interest-based ads and remarketing to children.
  • Apart from the technical details an ad needs, described above, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under 16 in the EU.
  • If you are a parent and think your child's data reached us, email orionlabstudios@gmail.com. We will delete it.

Who we share data with

We share personal data with two partners, and nobody else:

  • Unity Technologies runs Cloud Duel for us: sign-in, friends, parties and race connections. It acts on our instructions (Unity privacy policy).
  • Google shows our ads through AdMob and receives what an ad needs, as described under Ads.

We do not sell personal data. Under California law, personalised ads count as "sharing". You can switch them off at any time in Settings > Privacy choices, and doing so costs you nothing in the game.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect players and the game from fraud or abuse.

Your choices and rights

You can act on your data without writing to anyone:

  • Delete your Cloud Duel data: Settings > Delete my Cloud Duel data. This deletes your Unity account, your name and your friends list.
  • Change ad consent: Settings > Privacy choices.
  • Report a bad ad: Settings > Report an ad.
  • Stop ad tracking on your device: on iPhone, Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Everything else: email orionlabstudios@gmail.com and we answer within 30 days.

Your rights by region:

  • EU, UK and Switzerland:
    • You can see, correct, delete, restrict, move, or object to the use of your personal data.
    • You can withdraw consent at any time.
    • You can complain to your local data protection authority.
  • California and other US states with privacy laws: you can know, delete and correct your data, and opt out of personalised ads. We never treat you differently for using these rights.

Security, transfers and changes

  • Security: Cloud Duel sign-in travels over HTTPS, and race data is encrypted. We collect only what each feature needs, and restrict who on our team can reach the Unity and ad dashboards.
  • Transfers: Unity and Google may process data in the United States and other countries. Where EU or UK law requires it, those transfers use the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • Changes: if we change this policy, we update the date at the top. For important changes, we also tell you in the game before they apply.
