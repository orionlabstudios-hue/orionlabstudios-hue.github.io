Delete your data

Cloud Chase by Orion Lab Studios

Most of Cloud Chase keeps nothing about you anywhere but your own phone. The only data held off your phone is your Cloud Duel account, which exists only if you are 16 or over and have opened Cloud Duel. You can delete it yourself at any time.

Delete it in the game

  1. Open Cloud Chase and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Delete my Cloud Duel data.
  3. Confirm. The game removes your friends and friend requests, then deletes your Unity account and your player name, and clears the sign-in saved on your phone.
  4. You see a message when it is done.

Or ask us by email

Write to orionlabstudios@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Cloud Chase data". Tell us your Cloud Duel player name (it looks like Name#1234) if you know it. We delete the data and reply within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is not

DataWhat happens
Cloud Duel account, player name, friends and friend requestsDeleted from Unity's servers.
Your game progress, age group and settingsThese never leave your phone. Deleting the game deletes them, and they cannot be recovered afterwards.
Ad dataKept by Google only as its own policy allows, to deliver, count and protect ads. For players under 13 we keep none of it ourselves. See the privacy policy.
