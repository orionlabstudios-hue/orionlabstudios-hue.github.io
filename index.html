Support

Run the Road. Break the Loop.

Cloud Chase is a portrait action-runner with an 81-level story, made by Orion Lab Studios. Luna follows an impossible road above a drowned world, runs from the Keeper, and explores the places between runs.

Contact us

For help with the game, to report a problem, or about your privacy, write to orionlabstudios@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

Please tell us your phone model, what you were doing, and what you expected to happen.

Common questions

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. The story mode and Eternal Wake play offline. Cloud Duel, the online race with friends, needs a connection and is for players 16 and over.

Where is my progress saved?

On your own phone. Deleting the game deletes your progress, and it cannot be recovered afterwards.

Why does the game ask for my birth month and year?

Once, at first launch, so it can give you the right features and ads. Only an age group stays on your phone; your birth date is never stored or sent. See the privacy policy.

How do I report an ad?

In the game, open Settings and choose "Report an ad". It fills in an email with the details of the last ad you saw.

How do I delete my Cloud Duel data?

In the game, open Settings and choose "Delete my Cloud Duel data". The full steps, and how to ask us by email instead, are on the delete your data page.
